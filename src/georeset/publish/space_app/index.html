How to read this

Only the sentences labelled relevant to land use (landuse true) are shown, from every source or one. Every number is counted per H3 cell of resolution 5 (about 250 km²). Zoomed out, each larger cell shows the mean of its resolution-5 cells, so every place counts once however much is written there. Zoom in to reach resolution 5. Click a cell for its numbers, sample sentences and a scene.

The what places talk about layers colour each cell by its mean text embedding on three principal components, as on the dataset card: similar colours, similar descriptions.
